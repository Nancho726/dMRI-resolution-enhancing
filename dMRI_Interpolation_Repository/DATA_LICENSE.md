## Required attribution

When reusing the data, cite the associated research article and acknowledge
the dataset as follows:

> Vargas-Cardona, H. D., and Torres, C. (2026). Anonymized dMRI and tensor-field
> data accompanying the Python implementation of raw-dMRI and tensor-domain
> interpolation methods. Hospital de Fuenlabrada, Spain.

## Responsible use

The study is fully anonymized. Users must not attempt to re-identify the
participant or combine the data with other information for re-identification.
Redistributions must retain this file, the attribution above, and an indication
of any modifications made to the data.

The MIT software license in `LICENSE` does not apply to the files in `data/`.
